Beginner's Guide to Tennis 🎾



Tennis is a racket sport where players hit a ball over a net, aiming to land it within the opponent’s court and score points.


Basic Rules

  1. Players score points by winning rallies.
  2. The ball must be served diagonally.
  3. The ball must land inside the court lines.
  4. Players must hit the ball before it bounces twice.

Tennis Equipment

  1. Tennis Racket
  2. Tennis Ball
  3. Tennis Net
  4. Tennis Shoes

Basic Techniques

  1. Forehand
  2. Backhand
  3. Serve
  4. Volley
